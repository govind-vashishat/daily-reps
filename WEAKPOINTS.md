# Weak points

A mistake goes here once it has happened twice. Read this before every session.
Format: what goes wrong, then the problem numbers where it happened.

## SYS: processes, signals, streams, timeouts

## ASYNC: promises, cancellation, retries

## TYPES: unions, narrowing, guards, parsing at the boundary

## ERR: wrapping, causes, thrown vs reported

## REACT: state vs refs, closures, effects

## FN: functions as values

## DESIGN: designing before building

## COMM: explaining why
